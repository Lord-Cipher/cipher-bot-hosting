## 3. Blackbox AI (Coding Specialist)
*   **Role**: Bot Doctor & Advanced Coding
*   **Endpoint**: `https://www.blackbox.ai/api/chat` (POST)

## 4. DuckDuckGo AI (The Stealth Provider)
*   **Role**: General Assistant & Privacy Chat
*   **Endpoint**: `https://duckduckgo.com/aichat/v1/chat`
*   **Note**: Requires a `VQD` header, which can be scraped automatically.
*   **Pros**: Extremely stable, provided by a major company, anonymous.

## 5. Hugging Face Serverless (Anonymous Tier)
*   **Role**: Specialized Models (Niche tasks)
*   **Endpoint**: `https://api-inference.huggingface.co/models/{model_id}`
*   **Pros**: Access to 100,000+ models. Some models allow limited anonymous calls.
